What this tool does

Compare a measured or desired SU carburettor needle profile against the official tables. Ranks by shape (RMS) and annular fuel capacity. Use Direct compare to overlay any needle (e.g. TW vs SM) even if it is not in the Top 20.

How stations are measured

Measurements every 1/8" from the top shoulder. Tolerance ±0.0003".

  • .090" — stations 1–13
  • .100" / .125" — stations 1–16
  • 3/16" — stations 1–20

Understanding the result columns

RMS distance

Root-mean-square of diameter differences. Lower = closer shape match. Top 20 sorted by RMS.

Δ diameter (mean)

Average (candidate − target). Negative ≈ thinner/richer; positive ≈ thicker/leaner.

Max deviation

Largest absolute diameter difference at any station.

Fuel vs target (%)

Annular gap A = J² − D². Relative % = (Acandidate / Atarget − 1) × 100. Card summary uses total area over all stations.

Richer / Leaner

From total fuel-area ratio. Progressive lists are the nearest small steps, not large real-world jumps.

Worked example — TW vs SM (station 9)

J=0.100, TW D=0.0775, SM D=0.0735 → ATW=0.00399375, ASM=0.00459775 → +15.1% at station 9; overall ≈ +9.3%; RMS ≈ 0.00252 (outside Top 20 by shape).

Graphs

Overview (fuel): Y-axis scaled from stations 3 to (N−2) so near-jet extremes do not compress the mid-profile. Stations outside that scale are clipped to the plot edge with an arrow and true %. Full scale includes every station in the axis range.
