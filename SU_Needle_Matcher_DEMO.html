About this demo

SU Needle Matcher helps find the closest matching or alternative richer or weaker SU carburettor needles by comparing a measured (or desired) needle profile against the official tables.

This demo includes a small fixed sample for layout and workflow only. The full product contains the complete protected tables, full ranking engine, and authoring tools.
